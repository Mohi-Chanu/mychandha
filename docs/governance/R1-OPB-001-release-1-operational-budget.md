Status: Accepted for Phase 1 Gate D
Prepared: 2026-07-26
Accepted: 2026-07-26
Currency: USD

## Budget

The proposed Release 1 recurring operational ceiling is **USD 50 per month,
excluding tax**.

The current Gate D staging estimate is approximately USD 43 per month before
tax and usage overage. Provider checkout prices must be revalidated immediately
before external-resource approval.

## Governance

- This record is the single authority for the Release 1 operational ceiling.
- Gate documents reference `R1-OPB-001` rather than restating the ceiling.
- Tax is excluded; provider compute, add-ons, storage, bandwidth, and other
  recurring usage are included.
- A forecast over the ceiling is a hard stop.
- Changing the ceiling requires a material `CC-001` proposal and explicit
  approval.
- A later release creates or supersedes an operational-budget record rather
  than silently inheriting this amount.

Approval of this record does not authorize spending, provisioning, or
execution. Those actions remain separately gated.
